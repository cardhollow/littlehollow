Tap OPEN mode to reveal cells.
Use FLAG mode to place or remove flags.
Long-press a cell to flag it.
Use CHORD on an opened numbered cell when enough flags are placed.
MINESWEEPER
Open every safe cell without opening a mine. Numbers show how many mines are touching the cell.
PROCEDURAL BOARDS
New boards are generated procedurally.
The first opened cell is always protected.
If possible, its surrounding area is also protected so the opening is useful.
DIFFICULTY
EASY = smaller board and fewer mines.
MEDIUM = standard board.
HARD = larger board and more mines.
EXPERT = very large minefield.
EDITOR
The editor lets you create custom minefields.
You can place mines, force safe cells, choose a starting cell, randomize the board, save it locally and play it.
