Swipe up, down, left or right on the board.

Matching tiles merge into the next value.
2 + 2 = 4
4 + 4 = 8
8 + 8 = 16
And so on.
2048
Combine matching numbered tiles until you reach 2048.
GAME RULE
After every valid move, one new tile appears.

Only equal tiles can merge.

Each pair can merge only once during a move.
SCORING
Every merge adds the resulting tile value to your score.

The game continues after reaching 2048.
UNDO
UNDO restores the board, score and move count from before the previous move.

Only the most recent move can be undone.
