SUDOKU
Fill the 9 × 9 grid so every row, column and 3 × 3 box contains the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.
LEVEL
Level is separate from difficulty.
Completing a puzzle does not automatically change the level.
Use NEXT LEVEL when you want to advance.
DIFFICULTY
EASY = mostly direct singles
MEDIUM = fewer clues and tighter candidates
HARD = significantly fewer clues
EXPERT = very constrained puzzles
EXTREME = minimal-clue style generation
GENERATION
Each puzzle starts from a complete valid Sudoku solution.

Clues are removed while the generator checks that the puzzle still has exactly one solution.

The final puzzle is selected according to the requested difficulty.
MISTAKES
You have 3 mistakes per puzzle.
At 3/3 mistakes, the puzzle ends.
RESTART resets the same puzzle.
