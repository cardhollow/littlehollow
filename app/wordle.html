GAME RULES
Guess the hidden five-letter word in six attempts.

GREEN means the letter is correct and in the correct position.
YELLOW means the letter exists in the answer but is in the wrong position.
GRAY means the letter is not in the answer.

Every submitted five-letter guess is evaluated.
WORD LIST
WORDS.TXT supplies the possible hidden answers.

Entries are filtered to exactly five letters.
GUESSES
Any five-letter alphabetic word can be submitted.

The guess does not need to exist in words.txt to be evaluated.
SIX ATTEMPTS
You get exactly six guesses.

If all six guesses are used without finding the answer, the hidden word is revealed.
REPEATED LETTERS
Repeated letters are evaluated correctly.

A yellow letter is only awarded when there is still an unused matching letter in the hidden word.
